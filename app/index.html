Is it really a deal?

Every price on this page was read at the named store, or in a deals source that names the store, on 27 November 2026. The "usual price" is what the product sold for before the sale — not an inflated reference price. The verdict on each deal says what the evidence shows:

  • NEW LOW The lowest price we have seen for this product.
  • STRONG DEAL A big cut — usually 30% or more — off a stable usual price, or a large saving on something that rarely discounts.
  • GOOD DEAL A solid saving, roughly a fifth to a third off, or a meaningful cut on a product that seldom goes on sale.
  • ROUTINE SALE Under 20% off, or the first small cut on a brand-new item. Listed honestly, so you can see it is not a bargain.

How we check. Prices are read at the store or in a named deals source and written down with the time and source. A deal can sell out or change price after we check it, so open the store link to confirm before you buy.
